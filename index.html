Good to know

The details

Dress code

Black-tie optional

Please no black dresses or gowns.

  • No white, either — that's the bride's. Non-bride individuals wearing white will be converted to non-attendants.
  • Stone floors and lots of stairs (including from the parking lot down to the entrance). Choose shoes accordingly.
  • October evenings on the water get chilly — bring a layer.
  • The groom will for sure be wearing a suit. Potentially a cape.

FAQ
Which shuttle stop should I use?
Whichever is closer to you. The shuttle picks up at Ocean House Hotel at 4:15 PM, then Beauport Hotel at 4:45 PM, and arrives at the castle around 5:00 PM. See shuttle stops
What if I miss the shuttle?
Drive or take a rideshare straight to Hammond Castle, 80 Hesperus Ave — parking is free on site. Directions
How do I get home at the end of the night?
The return shuttle leaves the castle at 9:00 PM and drops off at Beauport Hotel (where post-wedding drinks are) around 9:15 PM. From there, take a taxi or rideshare to your hotel — book ahead if you can, since late-night rides in Gloucester are limited.
Are kids welcome?
No — this is an adults-only celebration.
Where are Nate & Rebecca staying?
Ocean House Hotel at Bass Rocks, 107 Atlantic Rd — also the first shuttle pickup.
Which airport is closest?
Boston Logan (BOS), about 27 miles away — roughly 45–50 minutes by car, more in traffic. The MBTA Rockport commuter rail line also stops in Gloucester.
