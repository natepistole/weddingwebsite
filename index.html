Questions
Which shuttle stop should I use?
Whichever is closer. The shuttle picks up at Ocean House at 4:15 PM, then Beauport Hotel at 4:45 PM, and gets to the castle around 5:00 — just before the 5:10 ceremony. See the stops
What if I miss the shuttle?
Drive or grab a rideshare straight to Hammond Castle, 80 Hesperus Ave — parking is free on site. Aim to arrive by 5:00 so you're seated for the 5:10 ceremony. Directions
How do I get home at the end of the night?
The evening wraps at 9:00 PM and the return shuttle leaves the castle right then, dropping off at Beauport Hotel (where post-wedding drinks are) around 9:15. From there, take a taxi or rideshare to your hotel — book ahead if you can.
What's happening Saturday?
Brunch at 10:00 AM at the Beauport Hotel, 55 Commercial St. After that there are no formal plans — we'll probably be hanging out around the Beauport and maybe heading to a local bowling alley. Join us for as much or as little as you like. Directions
Are kids welcome?
No — this is an adults-only celebration.
Where are Nate & Rebecca staying?
Ocean House Hotel at Bass Rocks, 107 Atlantic Rd — also the first shuttle pickup.
Which airport is closest?
Boston Logan (BOS), about 27 miles away — roughly 45–50 minutes by car, more in traffic. The MBTA Rockport commuter rail line also stops in Gloucester.
